Docs

Sign up

Signup is by invitation.

  1. Open the signup link in your invitation.
  2. Enter your name, email, password, and phone.
  3. Choose Sign Up.

Create a plan

  1. Choose New Plan.
  2. Enter a plan name and a destination.
  3. Choose Create.

Invite someone

  1. Open the plan and choose People.
  2. Enter an email. Choose a role if one is offered.
  3. Choose Invite.

Add an activity

  1. Open the plan and choose Add activity.
  2. Enter a name, a start, and an end.
  3. Choose Save.

Print

  1. Open the plan and choose Print.
  2. Choose portrait or landscape.
  3. Choose Print.
